Min's Media

Hello!! I'm Mingyo (she/her)—though most people call me Min. I'm an Anthropology and Media Studies student, who occasionally likes to dabble in a multitude of hobbies. I recieved my Associate's in Liberal Arts at LaGuardia Community College, and transferred to Hunter in Fall 2024; I only recently added Media Studies as a double major this semester! Given that I have little to no background in anything media related, I am open to learning new skills.

I've played around with many forms of creating art, ranging from watercolor and oil pastels to writing and photography. I've found writing in particular to be the best way to express myself, as I find it difficult to verbalize how I'm feeling most of the time. I've recently begun writing small thinkpieces, though those are stuck in my notes app for the time being. As for photography, I am a novice; however, it's great to be able to capture details from everyday life I find beautiful. I would say I am more of a hands-on person when it comes to art as a whole, as I enjoy the feeling of the different materials as I create something with my hands. Outside of anything art-related, I love coffee and hojicha! I'm always looking for a new cafe to go to, preferably one where I can people watch.
